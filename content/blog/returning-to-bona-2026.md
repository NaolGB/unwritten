A new school year brings new opportunities to learn, grow and discover what is possible. But for many students, preparing for that journey starts with something simple: having the essential materials needed to take part in the classroom.

On **Saturday, 19 September 2026**, students in Bona received school materials through **እንማር (Enimar)**, an initiative led by Rotaract Club of Hawassa to support students as they prepare for the 2026/27 academic year.

For Unwritten, this project was particularly meaningful. Following our first school material donation collaboration with Rotaract Club of Hawassa in 2025, we were pleased to support the initiative for a second year.

What began as a shared effort to provide essential stationery continued as an opportunity to strengthen our collaboration and support more students in their learning journeys.

## እንማር: Learning and growing together

The እንማር project brought together organisations, volunteers and community members around a shared belief in the importance of education.

Led by Rotaract Club of Hawassa, the initiative focused on providing school materials to students in Bona. Beyond distributing supplies, the day was an opportunity to connect with students, share words of encouragement and remind them that their education and aspirations matter.

![Volunteers sitting around a wooden bench on the grass, sorting exercise books and smiling](/blog/returning-to-bona-2026/volunteers.jpg)

Seeing students receive their materials was a meaningful reminder that supporting education does not always require extraordinary interventions. Sometimes, it begins with making sure a student has an exercise book to write in or a pen to take notes with.

## A practical contribution towards learning

As part of the initiative, Unwritten contributed **25,000 ETB** towards the provision of school materials.

Our planned allocation was designed to support 36 students, with each student receiving a full-year kit consisting of eight exercise books and eight pens.

The contribution was structured as follows:

| School material | Planned quantity |
|---|---:|
| Exercise books | 288 |
| Pens | 288 |
| Reserve exercise books | 36 |
| Reserve pens | 36 |

The additional supplies were included as a reserve to help account for materials that might be filled, misplaced or need replacing during the school year.

![Hands sorting piles of exercise books on the grass, seen from above](/blog/returning-to-bona-2026/sorting.jpg)

We believe that practical support matters. Exercise books and pens may seem like small things, but they are part of a student's everyday learning experience. Helping make these resources available is one way of supporting students as they pursue their education.

## The power of working together

Initiatives like እንማር are made possible through collaboration.

We are grateful to **Rotaract Club of Hawassa** for taking the initiative, coordinating the project and bringing different partners together around a common purpose.

We also extend our heartfelt thanks to **Ethioware** and **Ethio College Prep (ECP)** for their generous school-material contributions, alongside the volunteers and everyone who dedicated their time and energy to making the Bona project possible.

![The volunteers gathered in front of a Rotaract banner outside a green school building](/blog/returning-to-bona-2026/team.jpg)

It is encouraging to see different organisations contribute what they can towards a shared goal. No single contribution needs to do everything. When people and organisations work together, their efforts can reach further.

## Looking beyond the distribution day

Returning to Bona for a second year was an opportunity to build on the efforts of the previous project.

Supporting education means recognising the everyday challenges students face and finding practical ways to help address them. It means listening to communities, working alongside local organisations and building partnerships that respond to real needs.

For us at Unwritten, this is part of the bigger picture: creating opportunities for young people to learn, develop and imagine a future with more possibilities.

We know that school materials alone cannot solve every challenge in education. But they can help make the journey a little easier, and they represent something equally important: a community that cares about its students and believes in their potential.

## Learning, supporting and growing together

We are thankful to everyone who helped make the second እንማር initiative possible and proud to have contributed alongside Rotaract Club of Hawassa and its partners.

To the students in Bona: we hope your new materials accompany you through a year filled with curiosity, confidence and discovery.

And to everyone who made these initiatives possible, thank you for continuing to show what collective action can achieve.

Because when we support learning, we invest in possibilities that extend far beyond the classroom.

**እንማር — እንደጋገፍ — እንደግ!**

*Let's learn. Let's support one another. Let's grow together.*
