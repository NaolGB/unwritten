Education opens doors to opportunity, but every student's learning journey starts with having the right tools. Exercise books, pens and other basic stationery may seem simple, yet they play an important role in helping children participate in their classrooms and make the most of their education.

In 2025, Unwritten partnered with Rotaract Club of Hawassa on a school material donation project in Bona, bringing practical learning supplies to students at Bona Primary School.

It was the beginning of a collaboration built around a shared belief: supporting education can start with small, meaningful actions.

## Bringing essential learning materials to students

Through the initiative, 40 students at Bona Primary School received exercise books and pens to support their learning.

The distribution was organised to ensure that every student received a basic allocation of supplies, with additional materials distributed according to the project's allocation plan.

Each of the 40 students received:

- 4 exercise books
- 6 pens

Additional supplies were also distributed:

- 10 students received an extra pen each.
- 16 students received two additional exercise books each.

In total, all 192 exercise books and 250 pens purchased for this part of the initiative were distributed.

![Volunteers handing school supplies to a student, with a hand resting on the student's shoulder](/blog/bona-school-materials-2025/student.jpg)

The project also recognised the needs of younger learners. A further 20 students in Grades 1 and 2 received basic stationery to complement the exercise books they previously had.

Each of these students received:

- 3 pencils
- 3 erasers
- 3 sharpeners

These additional materials were intended to give younger students some of the basic tools they need for everyday classroom activities.

## Making every contribution count

The project was delivered with a budget of 15,000 ETB.

A total of 14,976 ETB was spent purchasing school materials, with the remaining 24 ETB used to cover the mobile banking transfer fee.

By carefully allocating the available resources, the initiative was able to provide different groups of students with materials suited to their learning needs.

For us, this was an important reminder that meaningful support does not always require large-scale interventions. Thoughtful planning and practical contributions can help address everyday needs.

## The importance of working together

We are grateful to Rotaract Club of Hawassa for collaborating with us and helping make the initiative possible.

![The Unwritten and Rotaract Club of Hawassa volunteers gathered outdoors around the donated supplies](/blog/bona-school-materials-2025/team.jpg)

Community partnerships bring together different strengths, resources and perspectives. Working alongside organisations already connected to the communities they serve also helps ensure that support reaches students in a meaningful way.

The Bona project was an encouraging example of what can happen when people come together around a shared commitment to education.

## A beginning, not an ending

Our 2025 school material donation project was more than a one-day distribution. It was the beginning of an ongoing effort to contribute to educational opportunities in Bona.

We believe that every child deserves the opportunity to learn, develop and explore their potential. While providing stationery cannot address every challenge students face, it is one practical way of making their educational journey a little easier.

This first project also laid the foundation for continued collaboration with Rotaract Club of Hawassa.

Let's keep supporting learning, one step at a time.
